# Trial conversations

Simulated participants talking to the live Off-Brand Reachy agent in text mode, through the app's real
Director (persona switches, distress, silence, senses). Made with `trials/run_trials.py`; the participants
are in `trials/participants.py`. No real people took part.

- **2026-11-18-run1:** 10 participants (enthusiast, shy, skeptic, Spanish speaker, uncomfortable, boundary
  tester, performer, kid, sad, chaos). Findings in [REPORT.md](2026-11-18-run1/REPORT.md).
- **2026-11-18-run2:** retest after the gaze, tone-of-voice, gentler-switch and persona-lens changes,
  plus a new "same words, different tone" participant ([tone.md](2026-11-18-run2/tone.md)).
